🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-07-07

1. HYBRID VEHICLES DRIVE Q2 SALES AS EV DEMAND SOFTENS
Automakers with strong hybrid lineups outperformed rivals in Q2 2026 U.S. sales amid high gas prices and cooling demand for fully electric vehicles. Toyota posted a 1.1% sales increase led by a roughly 20% jump in electrified vehicles, Hyundai surged 4% with hybrids up 67% in the first half of the year, and Honda reported an 8.4% overall gain driven by record electrified sales. The results underscore a market shift toward hybrids as a practical middle ground for cost-conscious buyers.

2. USMCA REVIEW OPENS WITH TRUMP PUSHING HIGHER U.S. CONTENT RULES
The mandatory formal review of the USMCA trade agreement kicked off July 1, with the Trump administration seeking to raise regional auto content requirements to 82% and U.S.-specific content to 50%. The changes would significantly reshape North American supply chains and could raise production costs for automakers sourcing parts from Canada and Mexico. Industry groups are lobbying hard as negotiations are expected to extend through late 2026.

3. COX AUTOMOTIVE CUTS 2026 U.S. SALES FORECAST TO 15.8 MILLION
Cox Automotive projects U.S. new-vehicle sales will reach 15.8 million units in 2026, a 2.4% decline from 2025, citing tariffs, tighter consumer budgets, and market fragmentation. The outlook reflects pressure from higher vehicle prices, rising interest rates, and the phaseout of federal EV tax credits. Analysts warn the used EV market will also see a surge of off-lease inventory that could further dampen new-car demand.

4. BMW iX3 CONFIRMED FOR U.S. SALE STARTING SEPTEMBER 25
The all-new BMW iX3 will go on sale in the United States on September 25, 2026, entering as a model year 2027 vehicle. The electric SUV delivers up to 434 miles of range and accelerates from 0 to 60 mph in as little as 4.7 seconds, positioning it as a competitive option in the premium electric SUV segment. The launch adds pressure on rivals such as the Audi Q4 e-tron and Mercedes EQC ahead of the holiday sales season.

5. CATL-BACKED BATTERY SWAP NETWORK REACHES 2,000 STATIONS
The Choco-SEB battery swap network, backed by Chinese battery giant CATL, deployed its 2,000th swapping station on June 30, averaging more than 200 new deployments per month throughout 2026. The milestone highlights rapid momentum for battery-swap infrastructure as an alternative to traditional fast charging, particularly for commercial and fleet vehicles in China. Industry observers see the network's scale as a potential template for similar expansion into Southeast Asia and Europe.

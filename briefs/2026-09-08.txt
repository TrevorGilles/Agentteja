🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-08-09

IVECO NEARS FINAL APPROVAL FOR TATA MOTORS ACQUISITION
Iveco Group expects regulatory approval for its acquisition of Tata Motors' commercial vehicle operations to be finalized by the end of August 2026. The deal marks a significant consolidation in the global commercial trucking sector. Once approved, the combined entity would expand Iveco's reach significantly across Asian markets.
FORD AND GM RAISE FULL-YEAR 2026 GUIDANCE
Both Ford Motor Company and General Motors have lifted their full-year 2026 earnings forecasts, citing continued strong demand for high-margin pickup trucks and SUVs. Ford's move comes despite a nearly 10% year-over-year drop in Q2 sales caused by a supplier fire disruption. Analysts see the guidance raise as a signal of resilience in the premium vehicle segment.
ZOOX TO START CHARGING FOR ROBOTAXI RIDES STARTING AUGUST 10
Amazon-backed autonomous vehicle company Zoox announced it will begin charging fares for rides on August 10, 2026. The move is a major commercial milestone for the robotaxi industry, as it signals a transition from pilot programs to revenue-generating operations. Zoox operates a purpose-built bidirectional vehicle with no steering wheel or pedals.
GERMAN AUTO PRODUCTION REBOUNDS, UP 3.9%
German automotive production volume rose 3.9% in recent months, driven by ramp-ups at Tesla's Berlin gigafactory on the Model Y, BMW Group on the X1, and Mercedes-Benz on the GLC. The uptick provides a positive signal for European manufacturing after a prolonged downturn. Industry observers note the recovery is still fragile and dependent on sustained EV demand.
SEVENTH-GENERATION CHEVROLET CAMARO DELAYED TO 2028
General Motors has pushed back the debut of the next-generation Chevrolet Camaro by one year, with the four-door version of the iconic pony car now expected in 2028. The delay reflects GM's broader recalibration of its EV and ICE product timelines amid shifting consumer demand. The new Camaro will be the first in the nameplate's history to feature four doors.

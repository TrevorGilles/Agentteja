🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-07-05

1. Honda CR-V Dethrones Ford F-150 as America's Best-Selling Vehicle
The Honda CR-V claimed the top sales spot in the United States for the first half of 2026, posting 226,114 units sold versus the Ford F-150's 209,311 and Toyota RAV4's 153,955. Ford's F-150 production suffered from fires at a major aluminum supplier, while Toyota's RAV4 faced inventory shortages during its redesign transition. The F-150 had led US vehicle sales for 15 of the last 16 years, making this a historic shift, though Ford expects a strong comeback in H2 as its aluminum supply recovers.

2. Tesla Q2 2026 Deliveries Beat Expectations, Stock Falls Anyway
Tesla reported 480,126 vehicle deliveries and 451,758 units produced in the second quarter of 2026, well above Wall Street's consensus estimate of around 406,600 deliveries. Despite the strong beat, Tesla shares dropped approximately 7.5% on the day of the report, suggesting investors had already priced in optimistic numbers or had concerns about underlying demand. The result nonetheless marks a significant rebound for Tesla, which has faced scrutiny over softening EV demand in Western markets.

3. CATL's Battery Swap Network Crosses 2,000 Stations Milestone
CATL's Choco-SEB battery swap network reached its 2,000th station on June 30, averaging over 200 new deployments per month throughout 2026. The network now covers 180 cities across 31 provinces in China, and CATL has raised its end-of-year target to 3,000 stations, up from the previously announced 2,500. The rapid rollout underscores China's push for battery-swap infrastructure as an alternative to conventional EV charging, particularly for commercial and fleet vehicles.

4. UK EV Registrations Surge 38% in June, Near 30% Market Share
Battery-electric vehicle registrations in the United Kingdom jumped 38% year-on-year in June 2026 to 64,440 units, pushing EVs to nearly 30% of the monthly new car market. Tesla was a notable contributor, posting a 42% rebound in UK sales after a difficult stretch, suggesting the brand is recovering traction in Europe. The strong figures reflect continued momentum from the UK government's Zero Emission Vehicle mandate, which requires a rising share of new car sales to be electric each year.

5. Hybrids Surge as US Consumers Cool on Fully Electric Vehicles
American car buyers continued to shift preference toward hybrid vehicles in mid-2026 as enthusiasm for battery-electric vehicles moderated following the expiry of federal EV tax credits. Hybrid market share in the US is growing at its fastest rate in years, while global light-vehicle BEV sales are still projected to reach 17.4 million units in 2026, driven largely by China. Industry analysts note that high interest rates are pushing buyers toward more affordable hybrid options, with loan terms of 72 to 84 months becoming increasingly common.

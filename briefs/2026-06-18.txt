🚗 Automotive News Brief — June 18, 2026

1. Rivian Cuts Workforce Days After R2 Launch
Rivian laid off several hundred employees, less than 2% of its workforce, just a week after beginning deliveries of its all-important R2 SUV. The cuts hit service, sales, and marketing teams as the EV maker continues pushing toward profitability it has yet to achieve.

2. BYD Launches Flagship Great Tang SUV
BYD unveiled its flagship Great Tang SUV after preorders topped 150,000, the highest ever for a single BYD model. The full-size luxury SUV offers nearly 600 miles of range and can recharge in as little as five minutes, underscoring BYD's push upmarket against legacy luxury brands.

3. Lucid Reveals Cosmos Crossover Design Ahead of Launch
Lucid registered the design of its upcoming Cosmos with the EU Intellectual Property Office, offering the clearest look yet at the sub-$50,000 crossover central to its mass-market strategy. The filing arrives months ahead of an expected summer reveal, as Lucid bets its future on cracking the affordable EV segment.

4. Ferrari Unveils First Electric Model, the Luce
Ferrari revealed the Luce, its first all-electric vehicle, priced at roughly $640,000. The launch marks a major milestone for the storied Italian brand as it begins electrifying its lineup while preserving its performance and luxury positioning.

5. Ford and Honda Issue Major Safety Recalls
Ford is recalling certain 2021-2026 Bronco Sport and 2022-2026 Maverick models over a front lower control arm ball joint defect, while Honda is recalling nearly 99,000 U.S. vehicles for a seat weight sensor flaw that could trigger unexpected airbag deployment. Tesla separately recalled 173 Cybertrucks over a wheel detachment risk tied to brake rotor stress.

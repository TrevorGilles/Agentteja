🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-06-24

1. Cars.com Names Tesla Top American-Made Vehicle for Sixth Straight Year
Cars.com released its 21st annual American-Made Index, with Tesla's Model 3 and Model Y topping the list again. Only 86 vehicles made the cut this year, down from 99 in 2025, as tariff impacts reshaped lineups ahead of the country's 250th anniversary.

2. EV Share of American-Made Index Drops as Tax Credit Sunsets
The share of electrified vehicles on the AMI fell from 30% to 24%, with EVs dropping from 11 models to just five. The decline follows the expiration of the federal EV tax credit in fall 2025, signaling cooling domestic EV production incentives even as overall sales keep climbing globally.

3. BloombergNEF Forecasts Record 23 Million Global EV Sales in 2026
BloombergNEF's Electric Vehicle Outlook projects more than 23 million passenger EVs will sell worldwide this year, an 11% jump from 2025. China remains the dominant market, accounting for 63% of global EV sales, underscoring a widening gap between US and Chinese adoption rates.

4. Honda Partners With QuantumScape on Solid-State Batteries
Honda and US battery startup QuantumScape announced a collaboration to develop solid-state battery technology and manufacturing processes. The partnership targets next-generation EV batteries with improved range and charging speed, joining a wave of automaker-startup battery alliances aimed at reducing reliance on traditional lithium-ion suppliers.

5. BMW Opens Orders for i3 50 xDrive Ahead of Autumn Launch
BMW began taking orders for its i3 50 xDrive First Edition electric sedan, which offers up to 563 miles of WLTP range. The car is set to reach the market this autumn, as BMW pushes to compete with long-range rivals like Lucid and Tesla in the premium EV segment.

🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-08-06

1. Volkswagen Announces Sweeping Restructuring: 100,000 Jobs Cut, Half of Model Lineup Axed
Volkswagen is planning to eliminate more than 100,000 jobs globally as the automaker battles a 6.3% drop in unit sales in the first half of 2026. The German giant is also slashing half of its group-wide model lineup in a dramatic bid to reduce costs and reverse declining fortunes. The move signals one of the most significant restructurings in VW's history and underscores the pressure legacy automakers face amid shifting demand and fierce competition.

2. Tesla Posts Strongest Q2 Ever with 480,126 Deliveries
Tesla delivered 480,126 vehicles in Q2 2026, marking its best second quarter on record and its first year-over-year delivery growth in two years. The milestone comes after a difficult stretch for the EV leader and signals renewed consumer demand. The strong results have bolstered investor confidence as Tesla continues to compete against a growing field of rivals.

3. Rivian Raises Full-Year Guidance After Launching Affordable R2 Model
Rivian exceeded its own sales targets and raised its full-year 2026 guidance to between 65,000 and 70,000 EVs. The company also officially launched the R2, its most accessible and affordable vehicle to date, targeting a broader mainstream audience. The moves position Rivian as a serious contender in the mid-market EV segment as it scales production.

4. Zoox to Begin Charging Fares for Autonomous Rides on August 10
Amazon-backed autonomous vehicle company Zoox has announced it will start charging passengers for rides beginning August 10, 2026. The milestone marks a significant commercial breakthrough for Zoox and the broader robotaxi industry. It signals growing confidence in the safety and readiness of fully driverless ride services for paying customers.

5. Slate Auto Unveils $24,950 Electric Pickup, the Cheapest in US Market
Startup Slate Auto has revealed a base price of $24,950 for its bare-bones electric pickup truck, a price point that has never existed in the American EV market. The announcement is aimed squarely at cost-conscious buyers who have been priced out of current EV offerings. If deliveries follow through, it could reshape the competitive landscape for affordable electric trucks in the United States.

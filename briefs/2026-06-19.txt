🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-06-19

1. Tesla shareholders vote on Musk's $1 trillion pay package
Tesla shareholders are set to vote on a landmark $1 trillion compensation package for CEO Elon Musk. The outcome will be closely watched as a signal of investor confidence amid scrutiny over Musk's divided attention across his companies. The decision could shape Tesla's executive pay precedent industry-wide.

2. Tesla's FSD faces EU regulatory tension despite new approvals
Tesla has won its fifth approval for Full Self-Driving in Europe, marking its second in two days. However, Sweden's transport authority urged the EU to reject blanket approval unless Tesla removes FSD's ability to exceed posted speed limits, with the issue going before a committee on June 30. The split highlights ongoing friction between Tesla's rapid rollout pace and European safety regulators.

3. BMW opens orders for all-electric i3 50 xDrive sedan
BMW has opened pre-orders for the i3 50 xDrive First Edition, an all-electric sedan offering up to 563 miles of WLTP range. The car is set for an autumn 2026 market launch and represents BMW's push to compete with long-range EVs from Tesla, Hyundai, and Chinese rivals. Strong range figures suggest battery tech improvements are continuing to narrow the gap with combustion vehicles.

4. Stellantis plans next-gen Opel Astra and Corsa on new STLA platform
Stellantis confirmed it will build the next-generation Opel Astra and Corsa on its new STLA platform, part of a plan to launch four new models by 2030. The move is central to Stellantis' European turnaround strategy as it consolidates platforms across brands to cut costs. Separately, Stellantis is partnering with Bolt and Pony.ai on a self-driving pilot in Luxembourg.

5. XPeng reports Q1 2026 financial results
Chinese EV maker XPeng released its first-quarter 2026 financial results, underscoring continued growth in China's competitive AI-driven EV sector. The results come as Chinese automakers expand more aggressively into European and other export markets. Investors are watching XPeng's margins and delivery numbers as a bellwether for the broader Chinese EV export push.

🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-06-16

1. Global EV Sales to Hit 30% of All Cars Sold in 2026
The International Energy Agency's Global EV Outlook 2026 projects electric car sales will reach 23 million this year, representing close to 30% of all cars sold worldwide. This milestone reflects accelerating consumer adoption and policy support across major markets, including strong growth in China, Europe, and the US. Around 150 new electric models are expected to reach showrooms during 2026.

2. Stellantis, Bolt, and Pony.ai Launch Self-Driving Pilot in Luxembourg
Automaker Stellantis, ride-hail platform Bolt, and autonomous driving technology firm Pony.ai are jointly launching a self-driving pilot project in Luxembourg. The collaboration marks a significant step in bringing robotaxi services to European roads, with the EU also announcing commitments to large-scale cross-border testbeds for autonomous vehicles. The move signals deepening cooperation between legacy automakers and tech-driven AV developers.

3. Mercedes-Benz Launches S-Class Facelift in India with Plug-in Hybrid
Mercedes-Benz introduced the facelifted S-Class in India on June 15, 2026, leading with the S 450e plug-in hybrid variant. The flagship sedan pairs a 3.0-litre turbocharged inline-six engine with an electric motor and a 22 kWh battery, producing 435 hp and 680 Nm of torque, with a 0-100 kph sprint time of 5.7 seconds. The launch underscores Mercedes-Benz's continued push to electrify its premium lineup in growth markets.

4. Nio Targets 40,000-Plus Deliveries in June as China EV Battle Heats Up
Chinese electric vehicle maker Nio has signalled that overall June deliveries will exceed 40,000 units, potentially its strongest monthly performance yet. The announcement comes amid intensifying competition in China's premium EV segment, where rivals including Li Auto, BYD, and Xpeng are all ramping output. Strong delivery numbers are critical for Nio as it seeks to reassure investors and defend market share.

5. Xpeng X9 Sets Range and Charging Records at El Prix Summer 2026
The Xpeng X9 electric MPV outperformed its official WLTP range rating by 11.4%, covering 401 miles on a single charge, and charged from 10% to 80% in just 12 minutes and 55 seconds at the El Prix Summer 2026 evaluation event. Both figures were the best among 24 EVs tested, highlighting rapid progress in battery technology and efficiency by Chinese automakers. The result is expected to strengthen Xpeng's reputation in European markets where it is expanding sales.

🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-06-08

⚡📊 1. IEA: Nearly 30% of All Cars Sold in 2026 Will Be Electric
The International Energy Agency's Global EV Outlook 2026 projects electric car sales will reach 23 million units this year, representing 28% of total global car sales. This follows a record 2025 in which over 20 million EVs were sold worldwide — one in four new cars. Europe led growth with EV share hitting 28%, while China's EVs made up nearly 55% of all domestic car sales.

🛻🚀 2. Tesla to Begin Base Cybertruck Deliveries on June 21
Tesla has confirmed delivery dates for its most affordable Cybertruck trim — the Dual Motor All-Wheel Drive — starting June 21, 2026. Early buyers have received notifications confirming their slots, marking a significant step in making the electric pickup accessible to a broader audience. Tesla also continues global rollouts of updated Model 3 and Model Y trims alongside expectations of volume production of Cybercab and the Tesla Semi this year.

🏎️✨ 3. Tesla Breaks Nine-Year Silence on Second-Generation Roadster
Tesla has issued its first concrete statement about the second-generation Roadster in nine years, confirming the company has begun planning the production layout for the long-awaited sports car. No launch date was given, but the disclosure signals renewed internal momentum behind the project. The original Roadster was promised as a halo vehicle to showcase Tesla's performance credentials at the top of its lineup.

🪖🤖 4. Harbinger Partners With American Rheinmetall on Military Electric Vehicle
Commercial truck startup Harbinger has announced a partnership with American Rheinmetall to develop an uncrewed military ground vehicle based on Harbinger's medium-duty hybrid and electric truck chassis. The collaboration brings Harbinger's commercial EV platform into the defense sector, where electrification and autonomy are increasingly strategic priorities. This deal highlights how commercial EV technology is crossing over into military applications.

🌿🇮🇳 5. Maruti Suzuki Unveils India's First Production-Ready Flex-Fuel Vehicle
Maruti Suzuki launched its first production-ready flex-fuel vehicle on June 4, ahead of World Environment Day, in a move aimed at offering cleaner fuel alternatives for the Indian market. Flex-fuel vehicles can run on blends of petrol and ethanol, reducing dependence on fossil fuels without requiring a full transition to battery electric technology. The announcement positions Maruti Suzuki as a leader in alternative-fuel vehicles in one of the world's fastest-growing auto markets.

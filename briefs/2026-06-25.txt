🚗 Automotive News Brief — 2026-06-25

1. Tesla, Sunrun and Renew Home launch 16GW virtual power plant
Sunrun shares jumped 26% after it teamed with Tesla and Renew Home to aggregate over 16 gigawatts of home batteries, smart thermostats, and other devices for utilities and data centers. The coalition already has 300MW available in Virginia's "Data Center Alley," growing to 500MW by 2030, as AI-driven electricity demand strains the grid.

2. Lucid cuts 18% of workforce in second 2026 layoff
New CEO Silvio Napoli's first major move cuts roughly 1,500 jobs and ends a second production shift at the Arizona plant, eliminating the COO role entirely. The restructuring should save about $158 million a year as EV demand cools, marking Lucid's second mass layoff in under five months.

3. Subaru takes $362 million EV write-down, pivots to mixed-line production
Subaru delayed its first independently developed EV and will instead build gas, hybrid, and electric models on the same assembly line at its Yajima plant, with Indiana and a new Oizumi plant to follow. The shift aims to reduce exposure to tariffs and swings in EV demand while leaning on Toyota-developed EV tech in the meantime.

4. Ferrari replaces marketing chief weeks after rocky Luce EV debut
Enrico Galliera, marketing chief for over 16 years, is being replaced by ex-BMW Italy CEO Massimiliano Di Silvestre effective July 1. The move comes a month after Ferrari's Luce EV reveal sent shares down more than 8%, though Ferrari says the departure was planned in advance.

5. BYD chairman sets 2030 target to overtake Toyota as world's top automaker
Wang Chuanfu told shareholders BYD aims to roughly double sales to about 10.5 million vehicles a year by 2030, surpassing Toyota's six-year reign. BYD currently ranks fifth globally with 4.6 million 2025 sales and faces steep hurdles expanding beyond China amid tariffs in the US and Europe.
